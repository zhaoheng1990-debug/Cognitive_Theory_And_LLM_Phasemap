## Editorial-language checks completed

- Replaced HTML-style citation markup with a plain-text reference callout suitable for the Nature enquiry form.
- Removed Markdown emphasis from the text intended for direct pasting into submission fields.
- Kept one factual result cluster in the enquiry summary: the three architecture/training/execution perturbation classes and the held-out boundary counts.
- Avoided stock promotional language, categorical novelty claims and generic AI-writing constructions.
- Kept all scientific ceilings explicit: the boundary is declared and task-bounded; it is not answer-correctness, open-ended generation or deployment control; the separability principle is an experimental design principle, not a claim of shared mechanism across systems.
- Kept authorship, preprint and competing-interest statements as send-time declarations. They must be checked against the then-current record before submission.

## Outcome

The presubmission enquiry is concise, editor-facing and readable outside mechanistic interpretability. The formal cover letter is intentionally distinct from the enquiry: it motivates editorial fit without duplicating the abstract or reproducing the reference list.
