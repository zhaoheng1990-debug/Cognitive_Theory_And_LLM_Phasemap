2. Complete the five send-time checks in `03_NATURE_SUBMISSION_FIELD_MAP_AND_CHECKLIST_V1R9.md`.

4. Paste the abstract and manuscript-comments text from `01_NATURE_PRESUBMISSION_ENQUIRY_V1R9.md`. Place the eight references at the end of the manuscript-comments field.
5. Do not upload a manuscript PDF or a separate cover letter for the enquiry.
6. Retain `02_NATURE_COVER_LETTER_V1R9.md` for a later formal Article submission. It is not a presubmission-enquiry attachment.
