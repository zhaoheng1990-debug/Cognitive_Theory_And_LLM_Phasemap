# Humanizer full-manuscript audit

## Scope and mode

Mode B, academic argument. The audit covered the Nature Summary, Main text, Methods, declarations and the compressed cover letter. It preserved all numerical results, equations, defined objects, model/checkpoint names, citations and claim boundaries.

## Revisions applied

- Replaced a cluster of formulaic summary and Discussion conclusions with direct statements of the experimental action and the supported inference.
- Reduced repeated contrast templates and abstract nouns in the Summary, coordinate-audit paragraph, architecture section and Discussion.
- Kept negative results and limiting clauses where they establish an inferential boundary; no caveat was removed merely for style.
- Rewrote the cover letter as a concrete editorial argument rather than a sequence of stock significance claims.
- Removed the personal acknowledgement of GPT/Gemini subscription support at the author's request. The AI-use disclosure itself was preserved.

## Findings retained without rewriting

- Methods use repeated boundary language by design. The repetitions mark distinct controls, non-nested selection limits, operator limits and task-family limits; collapsing them would obscure the protocol.
- Terms such as `\(\Delta U\)`, chord alignment, exact-initial, local-transition operator and prompt-condition classifier are defined technical objects, not style defects.
- The paper retains a bounded claim: it separates architectural propagation, training-organized compatibility and realized execution in the tested settings. It does not claim a universal dynamics law, shared biological mechanism or general output control.

## Post-audit status

No numerical, methodological or evidentiary claim was upgraded. No AI-generated-writing tell that materially impaired clarity remained in the Summary, Main text or cover letter after the targeted rewrite.
