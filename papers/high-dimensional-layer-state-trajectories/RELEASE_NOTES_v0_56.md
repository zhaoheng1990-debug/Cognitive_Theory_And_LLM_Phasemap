# Reproducibility release v0.56

This release synchronizes the full Nature Article submission package with three frozen closure audits. It is a source-data and executable-verifier release, not a redistribution of model weights, commercial/runtime binaries or large raw hidden-state arrays.

## Included evidence

- **Predecision coordinate audit.** The task-built `DeltaU_pre` coordinate did not improve held-out mechanism-label macro F1 over the matched recent output-logit margin in Qwen, Llama or Gemma. Complete predecision histories and the cutoff hidden state did improve over that comparator. This does not isolate an order-only effect.
- **Empirical-subspace direction null.** With layer, gate, action schedule and norm fixed, the structured actuator did not show a checkpoint-general direction-specificity advantage after correction across the three checkpoints.
- **New-graph geometry confirmation.** Qwen-1.5B and Gemma-3-12B-it-QAT-Q4_0 exceeded all 50 shared endpoint-preserving order shuffles in chord alignment on a frozen 240-prompt, 24-graph manifest. This is not a scale law or a functional-control claim.

The manuscript and Supplementary Methods state these results alongside their negative and boundary conditions. `code/extension_v0_56/claim_family_verification_matrix_v0_56.md` maps each claim family to its source data and verifier.

## Verification route

Run the three commands in `code/extension_v0_56/README.md` from the repository root. The coordinate and direction verifiers recompute released summaries from processed rows. The geometry verifier operates in processed-source verification mode unless archive-only raw arrays are supplied.

## Archive-only raw intermediates

`source_data/source_data/extension_v0_56/newgraph_geometry/raw_intermediates_manifest_v0_56.csv` records the SHA-256 hashes for the large raw geometry arrays. They are excluded from this public package to avoid redistributing derivative captures tied to separately licensed checkpoints. They are retained for controlled archival verification.
